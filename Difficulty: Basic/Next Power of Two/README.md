<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/smallest-power-of-2-greater-than-or-equal-to-n2630/1">Next Power of Two</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a positive integer n, find the smallest power of 2 that is greater than or equal to n.<span style="font-size: 14pt;">&nbsp;</span></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">If n is already a power of 2, return n.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 5
<strong>Output: </strong>8
<strong>Explanation: </strong></span><span style="font-size: 18px;">The powers of 2 around 5 are 4 and 8. Since 8 is the smallest power of 2 greater than or equal to 5, the answer is 8.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Input: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">n = 16
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Output: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">16</span>
<strong style="font-size: 18px;">Explanation: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">16 is already a power of 2. Hence, the answer is 16.</span></span></pre>
